The Covenant of Honor:
Terms of Artistic Stewardship

Last Revised: Reiwa 6 (2024)

I. Preamble II. The Artist's Vow III. The Collector's Stewardship IV. Digital Provenance V. Licensing Boundaries
I

Preamble

This document transcends the mundane nature of a traditional 'Terms of Service'. It is a sacred trust forged between the Creator and the Custodian. When you acquire a piece through Thi Bút, you are not merely purchasing an object; you are accepting responsibility for a moment captured in ink and time.

Let this covenant guide the mutual respect required to maintain the integrity, intent, and 'Ma' (negative space) of the physical and spiritual creation.

II

The Artist's Vow

  • brush

    Commitment to Craftsmanship

    The artist vows to execute every stroke with unwavering intent, honoring the tradition of their lineage.

  • water_drop

    Purity of Materials

    Only genuine Sumi ink, pure water, and archival-grade Washi or Kiri wood shall be utilized, ensuring longevity that spans generations.

  • air

    The Spirit of the Stroke

    The work is guaranteed to be an original manifestation of Qi (energy), never reproduced by mechanical means unless explicitly defined as a limited edition lithograph.

III

The Collector's Stewardship

As the custodian, you inherit specific duties to preserve the physical and conceptual purity of the artwork.

Preservation

The work must be shielded from direct, harsh sunlight and maintained in an environment of stable humidity (45-55%) to prevent the warping of Washi and the fading of delicate ink tones.

Honoring 'Ma'

When displayed, the work must be afforded sufficient negative space (Ma) upon the wall. It shall not be crowded by digital screens or visually clamorous environments.

IV

Digital Provenance

Each physical piece is irrevocably tethered to a digital Certificate of Authenticity (COA) residing on a cryptographic ledger.

fingerprint

The ledger acts as an immutable scroll. Transfer of the physical artwork must be accompanied by the transfer of the digital token to maintain unbroken provenance. Separation of the two voids the covenant of authenticity.

V

Licensing Boundaries

Rights Category Definition & Boundary
Private Display Granted in perpetuity. The collector may display the work in private residences or non-commercial private offices.
Reproduction Strictly prohibited. The work may not be scanned, photographed for commercial print, or utilized in digital media projects without a separate Commercial License agreement signed in Sumi ink.
Exhibition Public gallery exhibition is permitted and encouraged, provided the artist is fully credited and the 'Ma' of the display is respected.

Seal of Acknowledgment

By proceeding with acquisition, you press your metaphorical seal upon this document, accepting the profound responsibilities of a Thi Bút collector.
