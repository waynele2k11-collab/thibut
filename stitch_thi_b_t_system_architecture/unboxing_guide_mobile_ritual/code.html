Preservation

light_mode

Avoid Direct Light

Prolonged exposure to direct sunlight may cause the Sumi ink to fade slightly and damage the paper fibers.

water_drop

Control Humidity

Keep away from excessively damp or dry areas. Sudden changes in humidity can warp the mounting.

pan_tool

Handle with Care

Always roll and unroll the scroll gently on a clean, flat surface to prevent creasing.
