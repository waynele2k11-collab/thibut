The Seal (Hanko)

Establish Your Provenance.

In traditional East Asian artistry, the Hanko (seal) is more than a signature; it is the spiritual weight of the artist pressed onto the work. It signifies completion, authenticity, and profound intent.

Your digital seal acts as your cryptographic signature on the Thi Bút platform, embedding your identity permanently into the provenance of every piece you create.
