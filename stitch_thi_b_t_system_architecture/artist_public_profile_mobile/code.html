The Philosophy of 'Ma'

In Master Wei's practice, the empty space on the paper—known as 'Ma' (間)—is just as vital as the ink itself. It is the silence between the notes, the breath between words.

By emphasizing negative space, each character commands attention, allowing the viewer to experience a profound sense of tranquility and focused energy.
