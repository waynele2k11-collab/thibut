Materials & Provenance

Every stroke is an irreproducible event. I use only archival pine-soot ink from Nara, hand-ground on a century-old Suzuri stone, applied to heritage Washi crafted by living national treasures.

Each physical work is bound to a cryptographic token, ensuring the lineage of ink and paper is forever verifiable on-chain.
